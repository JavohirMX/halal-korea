About Halal Korea

Connecting Muslims to halal food and services across South Korea

Our Mission

Halal Korea aims to make life easier for Muslims living in and visiting South Korea by providing a comprehensive platform to find halal food, markets, and prayer spaces.

We understand the challenges of finding halal options in a new country, and we're here to bridge that gap by connecting our community with verified halal establishments across Korea.

What We Offer

Verified Listings

Every establishment listed on our platform is verified to ensure they meet halal standards.

Community Reviews

Real experiences shared by our community members help others make informed decisions.

Multi-Language Support

Available in English, Korean, and Uzbek to serve our diverse community.
